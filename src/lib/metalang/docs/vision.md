# Vision
Like lego bricks but for programming! A fun little playground inspired by lego that helps me enjoy programming again... but also dangerously powerful!

## Humanitarian vision
Why does this language matter? What's the point of it? Another framework? What makes it a language and not a library?

## Architectural vision
- Formulas instead of Functions
- Constrained creativity, but not tyrannical
- Everything is a flow, like english
- Varialbes are no longer graph-like, but threaded in the language, like human language
- Encapsulating stressful logic and esoteric symbols into small reusable building blocks, so I never have to write plain JS ever again.

## Redesigning core programming fundamentals
Programming contains these basic fundamentals from which all the other complicated things are created form...

## Playfulness & Ada Lovelace
you know which part makes me feel excited about this? the fact that every little method is a building block, a lego brick with it's own internal little circuit world. just like a little logic gate :~) and that makes me so happy becuase it keeps the childlike playfullness of being encouraged to build and click and assemble, but also take apart when you need to, and try something new. the possibilities are endless. and that's what makes lego so fun but so overwhelming if you don't have the childlike imagination. as an adult you see things more logically than narratively. a child, who is nourished and nurtured, has an imagination that is story-like. making little magical fantastical worlds out of sticks and figures and bricks and shapes... that's what each of these little methods are to me... not methods... not chainables... they are like lego bricks for programming. bringing back the playfulness and humaneness to code :~) let's be honest, we're not really bringing it back, playfulness in code was gone the moment people exiled ada lovelace just because she was a woman. but let's keep it optimistic and honor her work, and just like the programming langauge named after her, let's honor her in a little lego programmer kit, the lovelace kit! filled with a collection of little building blocks to do algorithmic computations in the style of the original programmer herself, lady ada lovelace! and that is the only thing keeping this project alive! that's ALL that matters to me :~)
