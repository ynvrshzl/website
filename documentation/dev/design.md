# Design: UI/UX and Aesthetics
UI/UX standardizations for aesthetics system of **"websys"**

## Aesthetic principles and inspiration sources
* __Apple:__ for it's emphasis on simplicity, bubbly clicky design that makes you feel like exploring and clicking on things.
* __Andy Matuschak's Notes:__ for his evergreen, zettelkasten, inter-connected note eco-system, while treating digital files, as organic, physical shapeable things. That's the core essence of the design for our web-system. Organic + Technologic.
