## Inspirations
Aesthetics, Principles and Inspiration sources for this project

* __Andy Matuschak's Notes__ — for his evergreen, zettelkasten, inter-connected note eco-system, while treating digital files, as organic, physical shapeable things. That's the core essence of the design for our web-system. Organic + Technologic.
* **[Apple.com](https://www.apple.com/)** —  for it's superliminal, clean design. Emphasizing readability over creative designs. Emphasis on simplicity, bubbly clicky design that makes you feel like exploring and clicking on things.
* **[Anime.js](https://animejs.com/easing-editor/cubic-bezier/inout)** — for it's incredibly well-designed system. integrating a whole-ass 3D model synchronised to a scroll event.
* **[Spotify.com](https://open.spotify.com)** &mdash; for it's sleek amoled black interface, and collapsible panels. giving a sense of spaciousness or context, when needed. which improves application accessibility, drastically.
* https://www.eveonline.com/ &mdash; for it's futuristic, universe-themes
